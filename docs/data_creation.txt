Column Name            Data Type    Generation Logic                                                                                   Role/Meaning                                                                
student_id             String       Generated as STD followed by a zero-padded number (e.g., STD001, STD002).                          A unique identifier for each student.                                       
grade_level            Integer      A random choice from 1 to 12. Lower grade levels (1-5) have a lower probability, while middle and high school grades have a higher probability. The student's academic grade level. Used as a core input for generating most other columns.
age                    Integer      Determined by the grade_level. Ages are randomly assigned from grade_level + 5 to grade_level + 6. The student's age, loosely correlated with their grade level.               
gender                 String       A random choice from a list of Male, Female, or Other with specified probabilities (48% for Male, 48% for Female, 4% for Other). The student's assigned gender.                                             
attendance_rate        Float        Generated from a normal distribution. The average (mean) attendance is higher for lower grades and decreases for higher grades. The value is clipped to be between 50% and 100%. The student's attendance percentage, a key factor in academic performance.  
avg_daily_study_time   Float        Generated from a uniform distribution. The range of study time in hours increases for higher grade levels (e.g., 0.5-2.0 hours for elementary school, 2.0-5.0 hours for high school). The average daily time the student spends studying.                        
homework_completion_rate Float     Calculated using a formula that combines the attendance_rate and avg_daily_study_time with some random noise. The result is then normalized and clipped to be between 40% and 100%. The percentage of homework the student is likely to complete.               
past_score             Float        A weighted score combining homework_completion_rate, attendance_rate, and avg_daily_study_time. The weights for each variable, as well as the amount of random noise, change depending on the student's grade_level. The score is clipped between 30 and 100. A hypothetical score representing the student's previous academic performance.
motivation_level       Integer      A score is calculated using a weighted combination of attendance_rate, homework_completion_rate, avg_daily_study_time, and past_score. The resulting score is then mapped to an integer motivation level: 1 (Low), 2 (Medium), or 3 (High). A categorical representation of the student's intrinsic motivation.         
use_ed_tech            String       A binary string ("Yes" or "No"). The probability of a student using ed-tech is higher for students in higher grades and with a higher motivation_level. Indicates whether the student uses educational technology tools.            
video_time             Integer      Calculated in minutes. The total avg_daily_study_time is split into three parts based on the grade_level and whether the student uses ed-tech. A random uniform multiplier is applied to add slight variation. The portion of daily study time spent on video-based learning.              
quiz_time              Integer      Calculated in minutes, as part of the split_study_time function.                                   The portion of daily study time spent on quizzes or practice questions.     
homework_time          Integer      Calculated in minutes, as part of the split_study_time function.                                   The portion of daily study time spent on traditional homework.              
preferred_learning_style String    A categorical label based on the clusters avg scores in the each columns 
quiz_accuracy          Float        Calculated based on a base score for the student's grade, with positive boosts from quiz_time, homework_completion_rate, motivation_level, and avg_daily_study_time. A random value is added, and the final score is clipped between 20 and 100. The student's predicted accuracy on quizzes.                               
predict_score          Float        The final predicted score for the student. It is calculated with a weighted formula that gives the most importance to quiz_accuracy and attendance_rate, and a small boost for lower grade levels. A small amount of random noise is also added, and the result is clipped to be between 0 and 100. The final target variable, representing a student's predicted academic performance.
pass_fail              String       A binary categorical label ("Pass" or "Fail") determined by a simple threshold on predict_score. Any score of 63 or higher is a "Pass." A simple pass/fail metric based on the predicted score.                     
dropout_risk           Boolean      A boolean value (True/False) based on a weighted risk score. Points are added for low attendance_rate, low homework_completion_rate, low avg_daily_study_time, low motivation_level, low past_score, and low quiz_accuracy. The student is a dropout risk if the total score is 5 or more. A binary flag to indicate whether the student is at risk of dropping out.
